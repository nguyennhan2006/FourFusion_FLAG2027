FourFusion — FLAG 2027 system architecture

Evaluation submission 1054704 · mean equal error rate (EER) 13.54 %

FourFusion · University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM

Each trial asks whether one face image and one voice clip belong to the same person. Two model families score every trial, and the Different Profile branch also scores English trials; a fixed rule per test file combines their outputs with fixed weights. Every constant of the system comes from training or external data, never from the test files.

Bengali filter

Applied to every trained component that sees voices. A clip is flagged when the top language is Bengali or Assamese, or p(Bengali) + p(Assamese) ≥ 0.2, under Whisper-large-v3 or VoxLingua107 (MMS-LID-4017 as a third vote). VoxCeleb2 and VoxBlink: every clip of a speaker with a flagged clip is dropped (608 and 1,637 speakers). MAV-Celeb v1/v2: the 58 clips flagged by Whisper-large-v3 are dropped. Faces are kept.

Not used: development data, AVSpeech, LRS3, any trial list.

Compliance

  • Every trial is scored independently, from its own face and voice.
  • No test-set statistics, clustering, cohort, score normalisation or test-time augmentation.
  • All constants come from training or external data.
  • Per-file rules depend only on the file’s language and protocol.

The pipeline in text

  1. Input. One trial: one face image and one voice clip. The organisers give each test file’s language (English or Bangla) and protocol (no-gender or gender). Every trial is scored on its own.
  2. Identity-classification family → ℓF. Frozen face encoders (organisers’ VGG-Face features, ImageBind-huge vision, ViT age estimator, FaRL ViT-B/16, CLIP ViT-L/14, CLIP ViT-L/14 with the identity low-rank adapter C5s) and voice encoders (organisers’ ECAPA-TDNN features, ImageBind-huge audio, audeering wav2vec2 age–gender, two SimAM-ResNet100, WeSpeaker ResNet293-LM and ECAPA-TDNN-1024-LM) feed seven scores: the bridge head (i)–(ii), the ImageBind cosine (iii), the age match (iv), the InfoNCE head (v)–(vi) and the shared-prototype head (vii). A face gender model (logistic regression on ImageBind faces, fitted on the gender labels of v4 training and MAV-Celeb v1/v2 faces), the wav2vec2 voice gender and age and the ViT face age give six trial conditions; with the English indicator they make seven. A conditional logistic fuser on 63 columns (7 scores, 7 conditions and their 49 products) turns them into ℓF, used by all four files. The fuser fitted for Bangla is also applied to English trials.
  3. Pair-contrastive family → ℓR, ℓR′. A linear projector over three face blocks and six voice blocks, trained with symmetric InfoNCE on faces and voices taken from different videos, gives a cosine and two log-norms. Its extra face block reuses the identity adapter C5s of the first family. Side streams add the ImageBind cosine with the audio adapter (C1), the age match and the gender probes. The gender-file fuser (4 streams) gives ℓR for gender English; the no-gender fuser (29 columns) gives ℓR for both no-gender files. For gender Bangla, voice block B (trained toward frozen face anchors from CLIP ViT-L/14 with C5s and FaRL) is added to the projector, and the projector and fuser are retrained, giving ℓR′.
  4. Different Profile (DP) branch → cDP, English files only. Two towers on frozen recognition encoders (organisers’ VGG-Face, AdaFace IR-101, ArcFace buffalo_l; organisers’ ECAPA-TDNN, SpeechBrain ECAPA-TDNN and its pooled statistics), trained on the v4 training set and MAV-Celeb v1/v2, give the cosine cDP.
  5. Per-file scoring. No-gender Bangla: s = ℓF + ℓR (equal error rate 11.97 %). Gender Bangla: s = ℓF + ℓR′ (18.99 %). No-gender English: s = (ℓF + ℓR) / 5.098 + (cDP − μDP) / σDP (10.20 %). Gender English: s = (ℓF + ℓR) / 3.838 + (cDP − μDP) / σDP (12.98 %). The four constants are measured on held-out training data and are the same for every trial, so they act as fixed fusion weights.
  6. Output. The submission stores −s for every trial; mean equal error rate 13.54 % (CodaBench submission 1054704).
